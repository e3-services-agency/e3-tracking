## Durable cooperation contract
The coordinator is the sole writer of .claude/team records. BOARD.md is append-only, one event per entry, with task and branch/worktree plus evidence. TASKS.md holds current ownership/dependencies/acceptance and TODO -> IN_PROGRESS -> REVIEW -> QA -> DONE. Implementers hand off; reviewers advance to QA or return to implementation; QA/coordinator closes. Decisions are recorded the same turn. Parallel workers never edit team files or another worker's assigned files.
Accepted tasks are committed and integrated into agent-setup after separate review and QA. Preserve the six existing deployment-related local changes; do not stage them with portal work. Do not overwrite, stash or clean another agent's work.
At every stop: exact branch/path, changes, checks/results, gaps, processes started and next action. Stop only owned PIDs. Secrets, OAuth JSON contents and live share tokens never enter Git, reports or tool output.
Routine choices/checks/fixes within scope proceed autonomously. Escalate only material scope changes, conflicting requirements, destructive actions or unavailable external prerequisites.
