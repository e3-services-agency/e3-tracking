# How to enhance a use case in the Use Case Center

## Email

* try different A/B tests
* consider changing the frequency of the emails sent out
* change the number of products displayed in the email
* test different email designs
* redesign the email by changing the subject, copywriting, improving CTA
* change sending time

## Banner

* show the banner on only specific pages
* try different A/B tests
* test different banner designs
* redesign the banner by improving the copywriting, CTA
