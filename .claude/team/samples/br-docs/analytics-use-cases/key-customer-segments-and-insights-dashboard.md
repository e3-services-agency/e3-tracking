## Use case description

[Key customer segments & insights dashboard](https://www.bloomreach.com/en/products/engagement/plug-and-play/key-customer-segments-and-insights-dashboard) delivers high-level insights into core business metrics such as customer lifetime values, average customer value, and conversion rates. This data-rich dashboard covers everything from highlighting potential prospects to encouraging repeat purchases from your existing customers. It is a simplified yet comprehensive solution for all your data-driven decision-making needs.

## How to deploy the use case

After downloading the use case, follow these steps.

### Meet the requirements

Check if the data in your project [meets the requirements](https://documentation.bloomreach.com/engagement/docs/how-to-meet-the-requirements). The Use Case Center lists requirements for each use case during the deployment process.

### Understand the use case logic

Go through the assets below and make the required adjustments. Find these assets in the Use Case Setup step.

> ❗️ Warning
>
> This utilizes several aspects of the RFM segmentation. However, the RFM segmentation itself is not directly part of this initiative.
>
> Please download the RFM Segmentation in the Use Case Center first.

* Definition of Purchase event - Adjust the purchase definition in the segment to match the successful purchase tracking in your project.
* Customer Segmentation 1 - Check the consent in all segments to verify that it matches the newsletter consent in your project.
* Customer Segmentation 2 - Adjust the consent to match emailing/newsletter consent in the segment ‘Unconverted Subscribers.’
* RFM Segmentation - Map the segmentation to the RFM segments in your projects. Deploy the RFM segmentation from the Use Case Center if needed.
* RFM Report 1 - Map the RFM segmentation in your project to the rows in the Drill down section. Deploy the RFM segmentation from the Use Case Center if needed.
* RFM Report 2 - Map the RFM segmentation in your project to the rows in the Drill down section. Deploy the RFM segmentation from the Use Case Center if needed.

> 💡 Tip
>
> Don’t forget to regularly check the results so you can optimize your campaigns for better performance.
